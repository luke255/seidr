Your Navidrome library on your Android phone, car and TV, laid out like the classic streaming apps. Downloads that look after themselves, kids' accounts you control, and play on the TV from your phone. Free, open source, and no tracking.

What it is

Seiðr is a native Android client for one self-hosted music server: Navidrome, or gonic and other Subsonic-compatible servers. Its look and layout follow the streaming apps at their best, with your own music. It's for people who want their own library to feel like a streaming app, and who want downloads they don't have to babysit.

Seiðr (Old Norse, a kind of sorcery) is said "SAY-ther". The ASCII spelling is Seidr.

Features

Offline you can trust

  • Downloads stay in step with your server. Switch on Download for a playlist or album and it looks after itself: songs added on the server download, and songs removed are cleared from your phone.
  • It won't lose your music. A dropped connection or a server having a bad day never deletes anything.
  • Original quality. Downloads are your files as they are on the server (usually FLAC), and anything downloaded plays from the phone, even online. To save mobile data, other songs can stream at a lower bitrate.
  • Edit playlists anywhere. Create, rename, add, remove, reorder and delete. Changes made offline are sent when you're back.
  • Smart playlists (Navidrome): make them and edit their rules, in groups that match all or any, with a sort and a limit. Every change can be undone.
  • Changes show up straight away, even ones made on another device.

Now Playing and the queue

  • Colours from the album art, so every album looks like itself.
  • Synced lyrics that scroll smoothly, with a countdown through instrumental breaks. Nudge them into time and every device remembers.
  • Gapless playback, gentle fades on play, pause and skip, ReplayGain volume levelling and a sleep timer.
  • "Next in queue" plays before the rest of the album or playlist. Drag to reorder, and turn shuffle off to get the original order back.

Everywhere

  • Android Auto, with recent music and playlists ready the moment you get in, and voice search.
  • Android TV, with every screen built for the remote.
  • Foldables and tablets, with two panes side by side.
  • Play on another device on the same Wi-Fi and control it from this one, or sign a new device in by checking a code on both screens. No typing passwords on a TV.

MilkDrop visualiser

  • Thousands of classic MilkDrop presets, rendered by projectM and reacting to your music in real time.
  • Adjusts itself to run smoothly on phones and TVs alike. On a Meta Quest, step inside it.

Kids' accounts

  • Give each child their own Navidrome login and choose what they can see and hear with three playlists on your account. Everything else stays out of sight, and their downloads follow your choices.
  • It fails safe: until a kid's device has your lists, nothing plays.

Also

  • Material You colours, a neutral dark look, or one of 16 colours.
  • In 26 languages: English, Catalan, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Czech, Danish, Dutch, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Romanian, Russian, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), Swedish, Turkish and Ukrainian.
  • Updates itself from GitHub Releases, with what's new. Obtainium works too.

Questions

Which servers does it work with?

It's built and tested on Navidrome. gonic works too (playlist changes show up on the next sync rather than straight away). LMS doesn't work yet, because it only accepts API keys. Other OpenSubsonic servers are untested. Jellyfin and Plex aren't supported.

Is it on Google Play or F-Droid?

No. Download the APK from GitHub Releases, or add the repository to Obtainium. Seiðr doesn't need Google Play services, so it runs on de-Googled phones.

Can it play music stored only on my phone, or from several servers?

No. Everything comes from one server and one login. Seiðr does a few things really well rather than everything.

What does it collect?

Nothing. There are no analytics, ads or accounts of its own. It talks to your server, to GitHub to check for updates (you can turn that off) and to fetch the visualiser's presets, and to other Seiðr devices on your network when you ask it to.
